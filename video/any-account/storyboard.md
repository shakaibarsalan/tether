# "Any Claude account" feature video: storyboard

**Goal:** a ~25 s feature announcement in minimal 3D motion graphics, about one feature only: one browser, any Claude account.
**Format:** 16:9, 1080p. The clips are Veo clips from Google Flow. The voice-over, a few words of text and the logo go on top.

## What stands for what

Every object stands for one thing, and that meaning never changes between shots.

| Object | Means |
|---|---|
| Laptop with a browser window **on its screen** | your Chrome, on your laptop |
| Round profile badge (orange / sand / off-white) | a Claude account |
| Padlock on the browser + a **key** | signing in |
| Tab cards with simple icons (mail, cloud, …) | the sites you're already logged in to |
| Orange cord from the laptop to a padlock | Tether: the browser stays tied to this laptop |

## Look (the same in every clip prompt)

> Minimal 3D motion graphics, soft matte clay materials, warm charcoal background, a single soft key light, gentle shadows, one accent colour: warm terracotta orange. Clean, premium product-launch style, slow smooth camera, shallow depth of field. No text, no logos, no people.

## Shots

There's no pause between lines. Each shot lasts exactly as long as its line, so every shot's action has to happen early in the clip.

| # | Veo scene | Voice-over | On screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A laptop on a pedestal with a browser lit on its screen. Three profile badges pop up one by one in front of it, and the browser glows each time. | "New in Tether. One browser, for every Claude account." | **New in Tether** / One browser. Any Claude account. |
| 2 | A browser window locked with an orange padlock. A profile badge arrives, a **key** slides into the padlock, turns, and the lock clicks open. | "Before, Claude could only use your browser from one account. Switch accounts, and you sign in all over again." | **Before:** one account per browser. |
| 3 | The laptop with the browser on screen. The three badges glide into the screen one after another; logged-in tab cards fan out above it. No lock, no key. | "Now, any Claude account on your laptop uses the same Chrome, with all your logins. No more signing in again." | **Now:** any account. Same Chrome. All your logins. |
| 4 | Pull back. An orange cord ties the laptop to a padlock on the pedestal. | "Tether. Free and open source, on GitHub." | Tether logo · github.com/shakaibarsalan/tether |

Sound: the Kokoro `am_liam` voice-over, with the clips' own soft ambience underneath.
